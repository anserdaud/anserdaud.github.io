RESEARCH & ACADEMIC PRODUCTIVITY

My research interests relate to cartilage treatment, sports medicine and joint preservation, spanning fresh osteochondral allograft transplantation of the knee, femoral head, and tibial plateau; osteotomies around the knee, periacetabular osteotomy with structural allograft for hip dysplasia; hip preservation in avascular necrosis and femoroacetabular impingement and meniscal root preservation in the multiligament-injured knee.

My research work has been conducted principally at Mount Sinai Hospital with Dr. Allan Gross and Dr. Paul Kuzyk who are world leaders in fresh osteochondral allograft transplantation, as well as at St. Michael’s Hospital with Dr. Daniel Whelan who is a leading voice in the treatment of various hip and knee preservation procedures including multiligamentous knee injuries and hip preservation in young adults.
